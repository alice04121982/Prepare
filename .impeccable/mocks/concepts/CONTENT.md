## What might stop
- Heading: What might stop
- Intro: Severe weather, a fault in the grid, a cyber attack on a water company, or disruption from a conflict elsewhere in Europe can all have the same effect at home.
- List of seven, each with the one thing that helps (these pairings are the site's own guidance):
  1. The heating goes off. Helps: blankets, warm layers, a hot-water bottle.
  2. Nothing comes out of the taps. Helps: 3 litres of water per person per day.
  3. The shops run short. Helps: tins and dry food you already eat.
  4. Cards and cash machines stop. Helps: some cash in small notes.
  5. Mobile networks and the internet go down. Helps: a wind-up or battery radio.
  6. Buses and trains stop. Helps: knowing who nearby you can reach on foot.
  7. Prescriptions are hard to get. Helps: a week's spare medication where your GP allows.
- Close: Most of these last hours or days, not weeks. All of them are easier with a few things in the cupboard and a plan you made while everything worked.

## Three things, in this order
1. Check what you already have. Most cupboards hold a few days. Tins, a torch, the medicine cabinet. Tick them off against the list before you buy anything. Action: See the checklist (/checklist)
2. Buy what is missing. Say who lives with you and the quantities are worked out for you. The free option comes first on every line. Action: Build your kit (/build-your-kit)
3. Talk to your neighbours. Two or three names and a way to check on each other. The better prepared you are, the more you can give to the neighbour who could not prepare. Action: How to start (/community)

## Why three days?
- Since 2024 the UK, the EU, Sweden, Finland, Norway and France have all asked their citizens to keep a few days of supplies at home. None of them say anything is imminent. All of them have decided it is no longer sensible to assume it cannot happen.
- Honest limit (said once): For the very worst cases there is little any household can do. For everything short of that, three days of supplies make a real difference.
