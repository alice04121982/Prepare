# Surface brief: homepage (and the site's visual world)

Scope: `/` first, then every page inherits the world. Visitor mode: Persuade on the homepage; Operate on checklist and build-your-kit; Read on why, worried, community, sources.

Audience and job: a UK household member on a phone, just seen alarming news, who needs to move from the headline to their own cupboard. Action: check what they already have (the checklist). Proof: specific, sourced quantities (3 litres of water per person per day), dated official guidance.

Chosen on 23 September 2026 by the owner after comparing four built homepage concepts (`.impeccable/mocks/concepts/`).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The household kit is a larder of labelled tins. Every hazard and every supply category is a flat, confident colour label with big lowercase words, so the page reads like a well-stocked shelf, not a civic dashboard or a startup landing page. Refuses: rounded cards, eyebrows, icon tiles, navy hero panels.

OWN-WORLD: Bright white ground, near-black ink, category colours owning full-width bands (water blue, food orange, power yolk yellow, health green, plus warmth and contact roles to cover all seven stops without repeats). Archivo, heavy and wide for display, lowercase headings; square-cornered controls with a heavy ink border; thick ink rules as shelves.

STORY: The visitor recognises their own cupboard, sees each thing that could stop and the one thing that helps, and checks what they already have before buying.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On a 390px phone, the question fills the screen in large lowercase; directly below, one ink button "Check your cupboard" in thumb reach, then the first colour labels peeking in at the fold.

FORM: Own-label larder, position 1 of 7 on the ranked list. Seed key: none (concept-seed not run; the skill's binary was declined, directions were ranked by hand and chosen by the owner from built mocks).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Open decisions

- Seven stops need distinct colours; the mock reused yellow and left two white. Extend the palette to six category roles and map every checklist category to one.
- Print version is untested; checklist and kit pages must print in black and white on A4.
- Imagery: none for now (start fresh); any future photography should look like the product's own shelf, not stock.
- Logo: none decided; the lowercase wordmark stands in.
