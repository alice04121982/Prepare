# Photo brief: Stay Prepared

Updated 23 September 2026. Replaces the earlier brief (Unsplash and Pexels
photos with a forest-green tint, plus Surface Pack illustrations), which
went with the old navy design and has been retired.

## Current position

The site uses no photographs or illustrations. The own-label larder design
carries itself on colour labels and type (see `.impeccable/briefs/home.md`).
The old Unsplash photos and Surface Pack illustrations were deleted from
`public/` on 23 September 2026.

## If imagery comes back

- Photograph the subject itself, not the category: a real cupboard shelf
  with three days of water and tins, a wind-up radio on a kitchen table, a
  torch in a drawer, cash in an envelope, a printed list on a fridge.
- Shoot flat and square to camera, in daylight, on white or a plain wall,
  so a photo sits next to a colour label like another tin on the shelf.
- No stock photography, no people looking frightened, no empty shelves,
  storm imagery, bunkers or tactical gear.
- Own photographs are preferred. If any image is sourced, record the
  photographer, source and licence alongside it and credit it on the
  Sources page.
